## 八、 历史财报市场反应复盘 (Historical Reactions)

> **市场归因规律提炼：** Alphabet's post-earnings market reactions follow a distinct asymmetric playbook: When management pairs elevated AI CapEx with transparent, empirical proof of top-line Cloud acceleration (>28% YoY) and expanding Cloud margins (e.g. 15%), the stock rallies solidly (+3.8% to +6.2%). Conversely, when CapEx expands without immediate Cloud operating leverage or when core advertising metrics show temporary softness (e.g. YouTube misses), the market swiftly punishes the shares (-5.0%). Going forward, the critical test will be whether Cloud operating margin can sustain expansion toward 18-20% and whether TPU v6 efficiency gains buffer the impending $18B+ depreciation wave as DOJ antitrust remedies progress through appellate review.
